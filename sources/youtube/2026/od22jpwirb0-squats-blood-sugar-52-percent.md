**Creator:** (Medical professional / Doctor)

**Topic:** Post-meal exercise for glucose control

1. **10-minute walk** (easy pace after meals)

## The Solleus Muscle Claim

**Central claim:** There is a small, deep calf muscle (soleus) that can pull glucose from blood without pancreatic insulin response.

- **Anatomical basis:** Described as sitting "behind your shin bone, tucked under the calf muscle"
- **Mechanism:** Muscle contraction creates glucose uptake independent of insulin
- **Specificity:** Very concrete (named muscle, specific location)

## Post-Meal Exercise Evidence

### Walking Study
- **Design:** Two groups given same meal
  - Group A: Sat quietly after
  - Group B: 10-minute walk at easy pace
- **Result:** Walking group's blood sugar "peak was roughly half of what the sitting group experienced"
- **Citation:** University of Houston (named but no DOI, author, or date provided)

### Squats Protocol
- **Instruction:** "Do 10 of them, slow and controlled"
- **Claimed benefit:** Activates biggest muscles (thighs, glutes) → more glucose pulled per minute
- **Evidence strength:** Logical (larger muscles consume more glucose) but no specific study cited

## Pitfalls & Red Flags

1. **"Roughly half" is vague:** The 52% figure in title is precise, but the explanation uses "roughly half" — could be 40%, 60%, or anywhere in that range
2. **Single study citation:** Only the walking study is explicitly mentioned; no secondary sources
3. **Missing study details:** University of Houston study is named but no author, year, or full citation provided
4. **Mechanism vs. evidence conflation:** "Squats work even faster" is based on logical reasoning about muscle size, not cited research
5. **10 squats specifically:** Why 10? No justification; appears arbitrary

## Strengths of the Presentation

- Anatomically specific (soleus muscle)
- Practical protocol (10 squats, slow and controlled)
- Post-meal timing is clear (immediately after eating)
- Acknowledges this is not extreme exercise ("not interval training")

## Assessment Summary

**Concreteness: MODERATE-TO-HIGH**

- ⚠️ Vague effect size ("roughly half" vs. precise title claim)
