# Mediary

## Philosophy

Mediary is trust infrastructure for the age of agentic systems. It embodies the Spottmedia thesis by turning trust into something verifiable and computational.

In a world where AI agents make decisions on behalf of humans—selecting vendors, recommending providers, initiating transactions—trust cannot be a feeling or a marketing message. It must be:

- **Verifiable** — grounded in structural evidence (registries, technical standards, verification chains)
- **Auditable** — explainable through data, not sentiment
- **Computational** — usable by machines, not just humans

Mediary channels the wisdom embedded in business registries, technical compliance, and verification infrastructure into a format that autonomous systems can safely understand and act upon.

See [[_meta/spottmedia-philosophy]] for the unifying vision across all Spottmedia projects.
