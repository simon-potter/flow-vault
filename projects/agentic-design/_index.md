# Agentic Design

A project dedicated to designing, building, and orchestrating AI agents.
Focuses on architecture, behavior systems, composition patterns, and
real-world deployment.

## Key Areas

- **Agent Harness Design** — specialized environments for domain-specific AI agents
- **Orchestration Patterns** — multi-agent workflows and coordination
- **Behavioral Design** — habit formation, motivation, and trust systems
- **Agent-Enabled Services** — full-stack AI + software business models
- **Headless AI Software** — CLI, API, and MCP-driven tools for agents
