## Core Pillars (Foundation)

The five essential elements that define an ideal life:

1. **A successful business under my control** — where I enjoy the people I work with, work for, or who work for me, and get satisfaction out of working on it every day
2. **Significant time in Australia** — a couple of months per year to spend time with aging parents without regret, while maintaining my life and connection to France
3. **More reading** — dedicated time to expand my mind through books, not just surface-level engagement
4. **Philosophy and personal growth** — seriously return to studying and researching philosophy, focused on becoming a better person
5. **Strong relationship with my daughter** — maintaining closeness even as she grows and potentially moves; staying close enough to follow her around the world if needed, building something stronger than I had with my own parents
6. **Financial freedom** — as the enabler that makes all other pillars possible

## The Freedom Gap (Critical Realization)

What's currently missing from my life:
- **Time freedom** — able to go to concerts and have full weekends without working
- **Relational capacity** — focus on friends and deeper relationships beyond work demands
- **Community and service** — participation in non-work activities, community engagement
- **Nature and presence** — working in the garden, connection to nature, doing this with my mum and connecting to her roots in Guerrilla Bay

**Key insight:** Time alone is insufficient. I need both time AND financial peace of mind.

## The Presence Principle

The deepest insight: **Presence requires both dimensions:**
- **Time freedom** — weekends/evenings actually off
- **Mental freedom** — financial cushion that lets me stop worrying about survival and opportunity costs

Without both, I'm physically present but mentally absent—thinking about work, financial stress, or missed opportunities. This undermines all the relationships and connections I actually want.

**Design constraint for the business:** It must:
- Not consume all my time
- Scale beyond my personal effort
- Generate enough cushion that I can genuinely disconnect when off
- Allow me to say no to opportunities without fear

This is fundamentally different from "maximize growth"—it's "build something that funds presence."

## Connection to Nature & Family

A grounding practice: working in the garden, connecting with nature, doing this with my mum, and connecting to her roots in Guerrilla Bay. This is both a priority and a method for presence with family across generations.

## Decision Filter

When evaluating any opportunity or commitment, ask:
- Does this move me toward or away from these pillars?
- Does it expand my time/financial freedom, or constrain it?
- Will I be present for what matters, or mentally absent?
- What am I saying yes to, and what am I saying no to?
