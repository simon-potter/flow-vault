## Concept

Every time you say **yes** to something—a project, a commitment, an opportunity, a priority—you're simultaneously saying **no** to something else, often without explicitly acknowledging it.

This tracker makes those implicit nos visible. When you're too busy to do X, you're not just "not doing X"—you're actively choosing something else over it. Over time, this reveals patterns: what are you consistently sacrificing, and what does that say about whether your life actually matches your stated values?

## Trade-Off Reflection Template

When you notice you're not doing something you valued, use this to surface the trade-off:

**What's not happening:** [The implicit no]

**What am I saying yes to instead:** [The commitment consuming the time]

**Which pillar is this affecting:** [Core Pillar from _index.md]

**Is this trade-off worth it:** [Analysis]

**What would need to change:** [To make this sustainable]

Example:
- **What's not happening:** Reading 2+ books per month
- **What am I saying yes to instead:** Business project requiring weekend work
- **Which pillar affected:** Philosophy & intellectual growth
- **Is this worth it:** No—this isn't moving me toward financial freedom meaningfully, just toward burnout
- **What would need to change:** Delegate or outsource this project, or change the business model to not require weekend grinding
