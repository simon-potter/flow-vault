# Raw Ideas & Observations

## Ideas

- **Better at how you do things, not just using the skills themselves** — e.g., meta processing. Don't just mechanically execute a skill; understand the actual goal and design around that. Think about approach, reasoning, trade-offs. Ask clarifying questions before creating templates.

- **When all software is easy to reproduce:** Be first, be fast, get in front of people. Then accept it will never be Microsoft. (The strategy in a world of commoditized building tools: speed and market presence beat perfection and feature-completeness. The winner is who ships first and gets users, not who builds the most defensible moat.)
