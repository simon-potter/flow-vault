# Calm

**Purpose:** Master remaining calm as a foundational life skill in my final year before age 50.

## Context

This is a strategic personal development goal. Calm is both a practice and an outcome—the ability to maintain centeredness, clarity, and measured response in high-stakes moments.

As I approach 50, investing in this mastery compounds across every domain: work, relationships, decision-making, health, and how I show up for others.

## Domains

- **Internal practice:** Meditation, breathing, nervous system regulation
- **In conversation:** Listening, non-reactivity, holding complexity
- **Under pressure:** Decision-making clarity, perspective maintenance
- **In conflict:** Holding boundaries without aggression, understanding opposing views
- **Uncertainty:** Grounding in what I control, accepting what I don't
