# Gratitude Reduces Stress

## Core Principle

Anxiety and stress over something you *cannot control* is pointless, even if the subject is important. 

Gratitude is the practice that **redirects attention from what you don't have or can't fix to what already exists and what you can influence**.

## The Logic

### Waste is a Choice

Stress over an uncontrollable outcome consumes:
- Mental energy
- Emotional bandwidth
- Physical resources (HRV, cortisol, sleep)

...and *changes nothing about the outcome*.

### Gratitude Breaks the Loop

Focusing on what you're grateful for:
1. **Reorients attention** (from threat to resource)
2. **Signals safety to the nervous system** ("we have what we need")
3. **Preserves agency** (energy available for what you *can* influence)
4. **Compounds resilience** (each act of gratitude trains reappraisal — the core skill of composure)

## Application

When stress rises over something outside your control:
- **Name it:** "This is not mine to fix."
- **Redirect:** "What am I grateful for in this moment?"
- **Notice:** How your body responds when attention shifts to what's real and present.

## Connection to Composure

Gratitude is a form of **cognitive reappraisal** — the upstream skill that separates genuine calm from suppression. Instead of fighting an anxiety or reframing a threat, you're genuinely shifting the frame to abundance.

This is sustainable composure, not suppressed reactivity.
