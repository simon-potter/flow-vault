# Scott Galloway's SpaceX IPO Prediction

## The Main Thesis

Scott Galloway warns that **SpaceX will hit a 10-year high the moment it goes public, then decline sharply after that**. His recommendation: **Do not buy SpaceX stock. If you do, sell immediately on the first trade.**

---

## Key Predictions

### **1. SpaceX IPO Performance**
- **Immediate pop:** SpaceX will hit a 10-year high (or all-time high) on its IPO day
- **Subsequent decline:** "It'll be all downhill from there"
- **Reason:** Massive speculative demand at IPO, followed by profit-taking and valuation normalization

### **2. Capital Flow Dynamics**

**Crypto Market Decline:**
- Crypto (Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins) is currently declining
- **Why?** Investors are liquidating crypto positions to raise capital for SpaceX IPO
- This is a **direct capital shift:** crypto → SpaceX IPO

**Broader Tech Stock Market Decline:**
- Tech stocks will also decline because capital is being sucked away by SpaceX IPO
- Investors are reallocating from diversified tech holdings to SpaceX
- **Galloway's assessment:** "So much capital is going to be sucked up by these guys"

### **3. Elon/Bitcoin Correlation**

**The "Cult" Dynamic:**
- Galloway describes both SpaceX and Bitcoin as having a "culty feel"
- They attract zealots and believers who treat them more as ideological causes than investments
- High-conviction followers tend to go all-in when new opportunities emerge

**Bitcoin Holders Funding SpaceX:**
- Galloway predicts that Elon supporters who hold Bitcoin will sell Bitcoin to buy SpaceX IPO shares
- This creates a **double risk:** Bitcoin holders will experience losses from both:
  1. Forced selling of Bitcoin (to fund SpaceX)
  2. Overvaluation of SpaceX (will decline after IPO)

### **4. Artificial Scarcity & Price Manipulation**

**Galloway's Dark Prediction:**
- "Elon and his banks will probably create enough artificial scarcity to manufacture a pop"
- **Meaning:** The IPO may be underpriced or structured to create a "pop" (rapid price increase)
- This is **intentional market manipulation** designed to:
  - Generate hype and media coverage
  - Drive retail investors into the stock
  - Allow insiders/early investors to exit at inflated prices

### **5. Valuation Warning: 94x Revenue**

**The Smoking Gun:**
- SpaceX is trading at **94x revenue** (if the IPO happens at current or expected valuations)
- **Galloway's assessment:** "Nothing feels or says froth in a market like a 94 times revenue valuation"
- **Translation:** This is an extremely excessive valuation that screams bubble territory

**Context:**
- Typical profitable companies trade at 2-10x revenue
- 94x revenue is reserved for:
  - Speculative growth stocks
  - Bubble-era valuations
  - Companies with unproven monetization models
