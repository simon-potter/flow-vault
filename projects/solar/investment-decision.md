# Solar Investment Decision: South France 2026

## TL;DR: INSTALL NOW

**Best value:** 6 kWc LONGI panels + 10 kWh BYD battery
**Net cost after aides:** €14,000–16,000
**Annual savings:** €1,500–1,600/year
**ROI:** 5–6 years (system); 10–12 years (battery alone)
**25-year gain:** €35,000–40,000

## Equipment Recommendations

### Panels: LONGI Hi-MO X6 (6 kWc)
- **Why:** 80% market choice, €1,800–2,000/kWc, 22.8% efficiency, 25-yr warranty
- **Cost installed:** €9,000–10,000 (with labor + TVA 5.5%)

### Battery: BYD HVS 10.2 kWh or Pylontech US5000 (modular)
- **Why:** LFP proven tech, 10–15 yr lifespan, €700–900/kWh installed
- **Cost installed:** €7,000–9,000 (with hybrid inverter + EMS)

### Financing
- **Éco-PTZ:** €15,000 @ 0% over 20 years (cover majority)
- **Grants:** €2,000–3,000 (prime + regional aids)
- **Out-of-pocket:** €0–3,000 depending on income
