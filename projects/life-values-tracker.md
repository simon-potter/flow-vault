## Purpose

A rolling log of reflections on what matters to me, my core values, and life priorities. A space to capture clarity on:
- What I actually want (not what I think I should want)
- What I don't want
- What I'm trading away with each choice (opportunity costs)
- Patterns in what brings fulfillment vs regret

This becomes a grounding document for evaluating life choices—when faced with a yes/no decision, I can reference these notes to see what aligns with my actual priorities versus what's just convenient or habitual.
