# I Let Claude Replace My Marketing Team. Here's What Happened

**Video:** [YouTube Link](https://youtu.be/-PFKQKZ3YB8?is=sdi6mxGCkTke6v4C)  
**Duration:** ~15 minutes  
**Episode:** Marketing Against the Grain  
**Format:** Live experiment + case study

---

## The Experiment Setup

### The Premise
"Every influencer is telling you that you can replace your entire marketing team with an agentic marketing system. But is that actually true?"

### What They're Testing
- An **AI Git repository** that went viral recently
- A company that **outsourced their entire marketing team** to AI
- Uses **skills and plugins** that Claude Code or ChatGPT can execute
- Question: **Can you really replace a marketing team (or portion of it) with these systems?**

### The Approach
1. Select a real **fast-growth AI company** (from RAMP data on go-to-market companies)
2. Run the **agentic marketing system** on that company
3. Evaluate the **actual output quality**
4. Determine if it's genuinely viable

---

## The Companies in Focus

Using RAMP's data on **fastest-growing AI/go-to-market companies**, candidates include:
- **Attention One**
- **Momentum**  
- **11x** (specifically mentioned as fast growth)
- **One Mind** (they're a user of this + Hotspot; ranked #3)
- **Hotspot**

**Selection:** One Mind was chosen for testing

---

## Key Insight: Git-Based Marketing

The agentic marketing system operates through:
- **Skills and plugins** (Claude Code/ChatGPT compatible)
- **Git repository structure** (version control for marketing workflows)
- **Automation workflows** that can be installed and executed
- **Outsourced marketing operations** (no human team required)

---

## Critical Questions Being Answered

1. **Does it work?** Can agentic systems actually produce marketing output?
2. **What's the quality?** What does the actual output look like?
3. **Partial replacement?** Can you replace *some* of your team without replacing all?
4. **Real business value?** Beyond hype, does it drive business results?
