# OpenClaw + Hermes Just Replaced My ENTIRE Marketing

**Source:** [YouTube Video](https://youtu.be/N5L1C1STZkw?si=Psod_oKS7plVOr-8)  
**Format:** Case Study / Results Demo

## Summary

Eric Osiu demonstrates how **combining OpenClaw with Hermes Agent** completely replaced his traditional marketing department. The result: 25,000 words of AI-powered SEO content produced with vastly reduced effort and cost.

## Key Components

### 1. Content Generation
**Tool:** ClickFlow (https://clickflow.com/)  
**Output:** 25,000+ words of production-ready SEO content  
**Format:** Long-form, optimized for search

### 2. Platform Integration
- **Hermes Agent** — Orchestration and automation
- **OpenClaw** — Multi-step workflow execution (complex logic, tool chaining)
- **ClickFlow** — Content generation engine

## The Replaced Functions

What traditionally required:
- Full marketing team (content strategist, writers, editors, SEO specialists)
- Multiple tools (analytics, scheduling, keyword research, writing)
- Weeks of planning and execution

Now handled by:
- Automated workflow via Hermes + OpenClaw
- Single integration point (ClickFlow)
- Days to deploy, continuous output

**Not explicitly stated in description**, but implied by "replaced ENTIRE marketing":
- Content volume increase
