# Marketing for Nerds

Automation tools and mechanisms that allow non-marketing people—technical people—to automate and develop marketing campaigns.

## Overview

This project focuses on bridging the gap between technical expertise and marketing execution. The goal is to document, evaluate, and leverage tools that enable software engineers, developers, and technical practitioners to:

- Automate repetitive marketing tasks
- Build and deploy campaigns programmatically
- Integrate marketing workflows into existing technical stacks
- Reduce friction in campaign development and execution

## Key Areas

- **Automation Frameworks:** Tools that allow code-first campaign development
- **Integration Points:** APIs, webhooks, and connectors for marketing platforms
- **Technical Workflows:** CI/CD-like processes for marketing content
- **Measurement & Analytics:** Programmatic tracking and reporting
