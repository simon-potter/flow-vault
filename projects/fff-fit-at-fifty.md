# FFF: Fit as Fuck at Fifty

Personal project to achieve peak fitness at age fifty.

## Overview

FFF (Fit as Fuck at Fifty) is a comprehensive fitness and health initiative designed to optimize physical performance, strength, and wellbeing heading into the fifth decade of life.

## Goals

- [ ] Define fitness baseline and assessment metrics
- [ ] Build sustainable training program
- [ ] Optimize nutrition and recovery protocols
- [ ] Establish accountability and tracking systems
