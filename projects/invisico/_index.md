# Invisico

**Status:** Live SaaS product (MVP shipped)  
**Type:** Decision-support application  
**Repo:** `git@gitlab.com:spottmedia/invisico.git`

## Product

A thinking environment where users describe a problem, and an AI council of 3 counselors (each with distinct reasoning styles) delivers perspectives + structured synthesis of disagreements.

**Core insight:** Cognitive friction between perspectives produces better decisions.

**Target users:** CEOs, founders, senior leaders making consequential decisions.
