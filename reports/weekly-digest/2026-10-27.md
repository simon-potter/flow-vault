## (4) Question Worth Asking Next Week

**What's the actual churn curve for knowledge-only vs. trust-based businesses in adjacent verticals?**

If knowledge is commoditizing, we should see systematic differences in:
- Customer acquisition cost (trust-based models higher? lower?)
- Customer lifetime value (trust-based stickier?)
- Pricing sustainability (who can defend price increases?)
- Competitive dynamics (more or fewer entrants?)

Examples to test: online education (course provider churn), consulting (project-based vs. retainer), SaaS (free-tier escape velocity), AI tools (switching cost to competitor).

**Why ask:** [knowledge-zero-trust-100] and [consulting-industry-dead] are both *predictions* of structural shift. Real evidence would let you spot which models are already shifting and which are lagging by 6-24 months.
