# The SaaS Playbook: Build a Multimillion Dollar Startup without Venture Capital

**Author:** Rob Walling  
**Published:** 2026  
**Subtitle:** Build a Multimillion Dollar Startup without Venture Capital  
**Foreword by:** Jason Cohen (Smart Bear founder, author)

---

## Overview

**Strategies and frameworks from the trenches to help you grow a successful SaaS company.**

The SaaS Playbook is your guide to building your startup without venture funding. It focuses on proven strategies and frameworks to take you through scaling from $5K-$10K/month ARR onward.

**The premise:** Most companies seeking funding don't get it. So how do you build a multimillion-dollar company without outside capital?

---

## Coverage (6 Critical Areas)

### 1. **Market**
- Strengthening product-market fit
- How to compete in a competitive market
- How much to worry about competition
- Building a moat (defensibility)
- International expansion strategies

### 2. **Pricing**
- Pricing structure strategies
- Freemium models: pros and cons
- Credit card requirement decisions
- When and how to raise prices
- Price optimization tactics

### 3. **Marketing**
- Finding more customers (the core question)
- Marketing funnels and their mechanics
- 20+ B2B SaaS marketing approaches (from The SaaS Playbook content mentions in related videos)
- Identifying which channels fit your business
- Sales demo structure and best practices

### 4. **Team**
- Team structure design
- Hiring managers
- Hiring great people (traits, processes)
- Equity, stock options, profit sharing models
- Co-founder questions: do you need one?

### 5. **80/20 SaaS Metrics**
- Which metrics to track (and which to ignore)
- 3 High / 3 Low metrics framework
- Churn: how much should you worry?
- Metric interpretation and action

### 6. **Mindset**
- Achieving sustainable success
- Focus and prioritization
- When to raise funding (and when not to)
- Turning speed bumps into roadblocks (and how to avoid it)
- Finding community and mastermind
- Avoiding burnout
- Founder retreats and self-care
