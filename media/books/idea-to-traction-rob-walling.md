## Why Now?

**AI changes the game, but not how you think:**

- AI lets you build software in an afternoon that took months to build (2010)
- But it doesn't stop you from building something nobody wants
- It just lets you waste time faster
- **The hard parts remain:** Finding a problem people will pay to solve, getting strangers to give you money, learning from customers
- These parts still take work; no amount of AI prompting removes the customer development step

## Key Insights from Related Video

**From [[projects/startup-ideas/when-to-quit-your-saas]] (Rob Walling, Oct 2026):**

Walling uses frameworks from Idea to Traction to help founders diagnose why they're stuck:

1. **Nobody has the problem** → Quit or educate market (expensive)
2. **You can't find customers** → Keep going, change marketing approach (most fixable)
3. **They won't pay** → Quit or pivot to adjacent problem (wrong market)
4. **You're solving it poorly** → Pivot solution (rare; usually one of above)

**The framework: 4 gates to product-market fit**
- Problem validation (does it exist?)
- Problem-solution fit (is software the answer?)
- Product validation (does your product work?)
- Product-market fit (can you market and sell it?)
