## Overview

**A step-by-step guide to launching a self-funded, bootstrapped SaaS startup.**

Start Small, Stay Small is written for developers with a day job and an itch to build something.

If you're a desktop, mobile, or web developer, this book is your blueprint to getting your startup off the ground with no outside investment.

---

## What It Covers (No VC Edition)

This book intentionally avoids topics restricted to venture-backed startups:
- Honing your investment pitch
- Securing funding
- Figuring out how to use piles of investor cash

**What it assumes:**
- You don't have $6M sitting in your bank account
- You're not relocating to Silicon Valley
- You're bootstrapping

---

## Core Focus: Marketing

**Key insight:** Most developer-entrepreneurs focus on building. This book focuses on marketing.

Why? There are many great resources for learning:
- How to write code
- Organize source control
- Connect to a database

But very few for:
- Finding your idea
- Testing it before you build
- Getting it into customers' hands
- Building a business (not just software)

**Start Small, Stay Small** fills this gap.

---

## Relationship to Idea to Traction

In 2026, Walling released **"Idea to Traction,"** which he describes as:

> "A spiritual successor to Start Small, Stay Small. It carries forward the founder-first philosophy with new frameworks, examples, and lessons for today's SaaS landscape."

Key differences:
- **Start Small, Stay Small (2010):** Bootstrap SaaS fundamentals
- **Idea to Traction (2026):** Updated for AI era, 16 years of new lessons, 240+ investments

**Progression:**
```
Start Small, Stay Small
  ↓
Idea to Traction [spiritual successor]
  ↓
The SaaS Playbook [scaling from $5K-$10K/mo onward]
```
