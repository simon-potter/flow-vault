# Research Outline Prompt

Used to create a structured research outline for deeper investigation.

## Template Variables

- `{{video_title}}` - The title of the video
- `{{summary}}` - The generated summary
- `{{focus_instruction}}` - Optional focus area instruction

## Prompt

Based on the following video summary, create a structured research outline for deeper investigation.

Video Title: "{{video_title}}"

Summary:
{{summary}}

{{focus_instruction}}

---

Create a comprehensive research outline in Markdown format that includes:

1. **Research Objectives** - What we want to learn

2. **Key Topics to Investigate** - Main areas requiring deeper research

3. **Subtopics & Questions** - Specific questions under each main topic

4. **Suggested Resources** - Types of sources to consult (not specific URLs)

5. **Methodology Suggestions** - How to approach the research

6. **Expected Outcomes** - What insights we hope to gain

Format the outline using proper Markdown with headers (##), bullet points, and numbered lists where appropriate.

The outline should be actionable and guide systematic deep research into the video's topics.
