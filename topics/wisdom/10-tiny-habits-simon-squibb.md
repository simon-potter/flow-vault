# 10 Tiny Habits That Make You Rich

**Speaker:** Simon Squibb

**Channel:** YouTube

**Duration:** 14:06

## Core Principle

**"If you copy someone, if you take someone's advice, you're going to end up with their life."**

Simon shares 10 habits he actually uses, demonstrating them with his lifestyle. The key: these aren't promises to make you instantly wealthy—they're habits that change your *relationship* with time, work, and wealth creation.

---

## Habit 1: No Music (Reclaim Your Thinking)

**Practice:** Don't listen to music while alone.

**Why it works:**
- Constant music fills your head with someone else's story and rhythm
- Without music, you activate your own imagination
- You hear what your body actually needs (rather than what a song makes you feel)
- Reclaim thinking time

**The shift:** Stop outsourcing your mental rhythm to songs.

---

## Habit 2: Work-Life Integration (Not Balance)

**Practice:** Stop separating work and life. Integrate them into one combined experience.

**Why it works:**
- "Work-life balance" is a lie sold to us
- When you enjoy your work, you want to include people you love in it
- He works WITH his wife, not apart from her
- Example: Turned his gym into a TikTok live shop (work + lifestyle in one space)

**The shift:** Stop saying "work stops here, life begins there." Make work a positive word.

---

## Habit 3: Nature Connection (Grounding)

**Practice:** Take off shoes and socks. Touch the ground daily.

**Why it works:**
- Humans evolved in tribes of ~500, connected through nature
- We lost connection when we started wearing shoes and living indoors
- This isn't health advice—it's about feeling connected to the world
- Similar to Habit 1: remove artificial inputs and reconnect to reality

**The shift:** Feel your connection to the physical world.

---

## Habit 4: Embrace Pain (Daily Sauna)

**Practice:** Regularly do something uncomfortable (e.g., sauna daily).

**Why it works:**
- Pain shows you're growing
- Others avoid what you can handle → competitive advantage
- "You have to learn to not be happy" — happy is fleeting
- What you actually want: challenges, excitement, highs and lows
- Not passive income; real success requires putting yourself through pain

**Key insight:** Building a business isn't easy. It's not meant to be. Pain is the price of success.

---

## Habit 5: Open a Business Bank Account (Immediate Mindset Shift)

**Practice:** As soon as you build anything, open a business bank account for it.

**Why it works:**
- Instantly signals "this is a business, not a hobby"
- Allows tax deductions (travel, expenses)
- Everything goes through one central place
- You can claim business expenses back
- Mental shift: thinking like a business owner vs consumer

**Bonus:** Use referral codes (e.g., Tide Banking offers £50 for £100 spend with code "Simon").

---

## Habit 6: Buy Time, Don't Sell It

**Practice:** Shift from selling hours to buying time from others.

**Why it works:**
- Time is finite; you don't have much of it
- Most people sell time (teachers, employees, standard 9-5)
- The 9-5 system taught us time = value
- **Key**: Only do things you enjoy

**Strategy:**
- Hire Diana (or someone) to do things you DON'T like
- She loves what you hate → win-win
- Spend 90% of your time doing what most people don't
- **Critical rule**: Only sell outcomes, never sell time again

**The shift:** "I'm not paying for your time; I'm buying freedom from tasks I don't enjoy doing."

---

## Habit 7: YouTube as Education Tool (Not Entertainment)

**Practice:** Use YouTube systematically for learning, not doomscrolling.

**Why it works:**
- YouTube is "one of the most amazing education tools in the world"
- You can access knowledge from experts globally
- Most people misuse it (negativity, entertainment)
- Shift mindset: YouTube = university, not Netflix

---

## Habit 8: Copy the System, Not the Person

**Principle:** Don't copy someone's life—understand their system and adapt it.

**Why it matters:**
- Taking someone's advice often means ending up with their life, not yours
- Simon shows his house, his life, his lifestyle not to copy but to understand
- The system (habits, decisions, priorities) matters; the specific outputs (house, car) are personal

---

## Habit 9: Structure Yourself as a Business

**Practice:** Think like a business in every area of life.

**Why it works:**
- You structure spending through a central account
- Everything is deductible
- You think in terms of ROI, not just expense
- Mental framework shifts from consumer to entrepreneur

---

## Habit 10: Skill Stacking & Continuous Learning

**Principle:** Don't rely on one skill or income stream.

**Why it works:**
- Technology, markets, and opportunities change
- Wealth comes from combining multiple valuable skills
- Always be learning, always be adapting
- The richer you are, the more diverse your knowledge

---

## Key Takeaways

### The Meta-Principle: Shift Your Identity

These aren't quick fixes; they're identity shifts.

- Stop being a time-seller → become a time-buyer
- Stop being a consumer → become a business operator
- Stop following systems → create systems
- Stop outsourcing thinking → reclaim it
- Stop avoiding pain → embrace it as proof of growth

### Why This Works

Simon's wealth didn't come from one habit. It came from stacking all of these over years:
- Refused to sell time
- Built businesses (opened bank accounts for every idea)
- Hired people who loved what he hated
- Spent time learning and thinking (no music distraction)
- Connected to real work (work + life integration)
- Embraced discomfort (sauna daily, building businesses)

### The Real Wisdom

"If you want to be rich, don't copy my life. Copy my system. Then build your own life from it."

The habits are tools. Your application is unique.

## Actionable Starting Point

**This week:**
1. If you have an idea: **Open a business bank account** (Habit 5)
2. Identify one task you hate: **Delegate or eliminate it** (Habit 6)
3. One day: **Go without music** and notice what you think (Habit 1)
4. **Watch a YouTube tutorial** on something that could improve your business (Habit 7)

**Over 3 months:**
- Build all 10 habits into your weekly routine
- Track what changes in your mindset
- Measure outcomes, not time spent

**The real shift:** From "How do I make more money?" to "How do I structure my life so wealth is inevitable?"
