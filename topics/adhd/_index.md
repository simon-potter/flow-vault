# ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder: neurobiological regulation challenges affecting focus, impulse control, dopamine, and executive function. Managing ADHD effectively requires root-cause approaches beyond medication alone, including environmental systems and intentional stimulation strategies.

---

## Quick Reference

**Core Challenge:** ADHD brains are regulation problems, not just focus problems. Dysregulation affects attention, impulses, motivation, dopamine, and emotional resilience.

**Key Morning Protocol:** Movement within 1 hour of waking (not necessarily intense—5–10 min works) provides intentional stimulation that prevents chaotic attention-seeking later.

**System > Motivation:** ADHD brains respond better to automated habits and environmental design than willpower-dependent routines.

## Core Principles

### 1. Regulation Over Discipline

ADHD mornings fail not from lack of willpower but from dysregulation. The brain wakes underpowered and immediately seeks stimulation (phone, email, conflict). Providing intentional, clean stimulation (movement) redirects this seeking in a healthy direction.

### 2. Circadian Rhythm Delays Are Real

ADHD populations show higher rates of:
- Delayed sleep phase (sleep + wake timing shifted 2+ hours later than average)
- Delayed melatonin onset (~1.5 hours later in ADHD adults)
- Later chronotype (naturally more awake at night)

This is not laziness—your brain and body are genuinely less ready to regulate early in the day.

### 3. The Morning Window Is Critical

The first 1–2 hours after waking set the day's trajectory:
- **With movement/activation:** brain gets regulated stimulus early, less chaotic seeking later
- **Without it:** brain spends the day chasing stimulation in unhelpful places (screens, urgency, reactivity)

### 4. Embodiment Matters

Many ADHD brains live "in the head"—scattered, fast, disconnected from body. Movement anchors attention in the body, reducing mental chaos and improving emotional resilience from the start.

### 5. Systems Over Motivation

ADHD brains negotiate daily with motivation. Build the environment to make the healthy choice automatic:
- Put shoes out the night before
- Use the same playlist every morning
- Place SAD light where you'll use it
- Chain movement to existing habits (coffee, meditation, walk)
- Don't depend on how inspired you feel

## Evidence Base

**Exercise & Executive Function:**
- Physical activity supports attention, inhibitory control, and executive function in ADHD (meta-analyses support this as a non-pharmacologic tool)
- Exercise increases catecholamines (dopamine, norepinephrine) → improved alertness, focus, mental organization

**Sleep Disruption:**
- High prevalence in ADHD populations
- Sleep disruption amplifies core ADHD symptoms and functional impairment
- Poor sleep worsens dopamine regulation and executive function

**Delayed Sleep Phase:**
- One of the most common sleep patterns in ADHD (per recent reviews)
- Melatonin onset delayed ~1.5 hours in ADHD adults vs. non-ADHD

## Common ADHD Morning Patterns (Dysregulation)

| Experience | What's Happening |
|---|---|
| Wake foggy, underpowered, already behind | Brain waking under-stimulated and dysregulated |
| Restless but unfocused, tired but stimulation-seeking | Dopamine seeking novelty/urgency/intensity |
| Immediate reach for phone, email, conflict, caffeine | Brain hunting for stimulation in unhelpful places |
| "Revenge bedtime procrastination" (staying up when tired) | First quiet time of day; brain finally feels awake at night |
| Cycle of poor sleep → worse dysregulation next morning | Sleep deprivation + dysregulation amplify each other |

## Movement Guidance (Not a Perfect Routine)

### What Counts
- 5–10 min walk
- Few songs dancing in kitchen
- Jog around the block
- Stairs, jumping jacks, air squats
- Stretching + light exposure
- Dog walk (without phone)
- Treadmill walk

### What Doesn't Matter
- Duration (5 min is fine; 45 min is overkill)
- Intensity (doesn't need to be impressive)
- Perfection (missed days or 3-min sessions still count)

### The Core Goal
**Signal, not performance.** Message to brain: *Here's your stimulation, your activation, your reset. Now regulate.*

### Once Implemented
- People doomscroll less
- Feel less edgy, more steady
- Easier time focusing
- Less emotional reactivity
- Day feels less like it's happening *to* them
